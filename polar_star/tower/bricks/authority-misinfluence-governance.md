## 原文（Talk Eleven 倾向22）
结构基础："Living in dominance hierarchies as he does, like all his ancestors before him, man was born mostly to follow leaders, with only a few people doing the leading."

耳滴剂："A physician left a written order for a nurse treating an earache, as follows: 'Two drops, twice a day, r. ear.' The nurse then directed the patient to turn over and put the eardrops in his anus."

副驾驶训练："copilots in simulator exercises will too often allow the simulated plane to crash because of some extreme and perfectly obvious simulated error of the chief pilot"——模拟训练专门教副驾驶"无视机长的愚蠢指令"，训完仍常失败。巴菲特版："cases like this one get the attention of careful thinkers like Boss Buffett, who always acts like an over-quiet mouse around his pilots."

米尔格拉姆："65 percent of his subjects, ordinary residents of New Haven, were willing to give apparently harmful electric shocks to a pitifully protesting victim simply because a scientific authority commanded them to." 芒格判词：任何拿着25倾向清单的聪明人一眼能看出该实验"至少六条强倾向合流"，而心理学界用了一千篇论文才理解九成。

钓鱼向导："the guide said in English, 'Give him the rod, give him the rod.' Well, the angler threw his expensive rod at the fish, and when last seen, it was going down the Rio Colorado toward the ocean. This example shows how powerful is the tendency to go along with an authority figure and how it can turn one's brain into mush."

折旧储备："A psychology PhD once became a CEO of a major company and went wild... 'Take the money out of the depreciation reserves,' said the CEO. Not too easy, because a depreciation reserve is a liability account."

判词："The obvious implication: Be careful whom you appoint to power, because a dominant authority figure will often be hard to remove, aided as he will be by authority-misinfluence tendency."

董事会的不作为（倾向15交叉）："the outside directors on a corporate board usually display the near ultimate form of inaction. They fail to object to anything much short of an axe murder... 'They asked me if I wanted to become a director of Northwest Bell, and it was the last thing they ever asked me.'"

## 断言
权威误导不是服从过度这么简单，而是三层复合：个体层（大脑对权威指令的解码优先于常识核验——r. ear都能滴进肛门）、组织层（撤换强势领导的成本因全员权威倾向而指数放大，哪怕他命令从负债科目里拿钱）、制度层（董事会不作为=权威误导×社会证明的乘积）。所以权力任命是准单向门：错的人上去就很难下来。

## 投资迁移
- 强人股尽调三问：这个组织里谁能撤换他？历史上有没有人成功挑战过他？继任者画像存在吗（管理半径是否已超过一个人的认知带宽）？三问全空=权威误导的结构性敞口，估值里记治理折扣。
- 信息解压纪律：管理层（尤其是charismatic创始人）的表述先脱权威再评估——同一句话从IR专员嘴里说出来还成立吗；CEO访谈的精彩度与面签印象同源（self-regard-endowment-bias），降权处理。
- 内部人挑战记录：高管离职潮/独董辞任/审计师更换按"谁在挑战权威、下场如何"读取——波斯信使综合征的姊妹证据。
- 对AI系统同样适用：模型输出在用户侧自带权威光环（尤其引用具体数字时），本系统的机器结论（量化否决/红旗）必须保留可解释的否决理由，让人能执行"副驾驶式质疑"。
