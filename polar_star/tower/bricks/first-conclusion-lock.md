## 原文（Talk Eleven 倾向4+5）
人卵机制（Keynes）："What Keynes was reporting is that the human mind works a lot like the human egg. When one sperm gets into a human egg, there's an automatic shut-off device that bars any other sperm from getting in. The human mind tends strongly toward the same sort of result. And so, people tend to accumulate large mental holdings of fixed conclusions and attitudes that are not often reexamined or changed, even though there is plenty of good evidence that they are wrong."

凯恩斯本人："it was not the intrinsic difficulty of new ideas that prevented their acceptance. Instead, the new ideas were not accepted because they were inconsistent with old ideas in place."

习惯锁链（Dickens）："When Marley's miserable ghost says, 'I wear the chains I forged in life,' he is talking about the chains of habit that were too light to be felt before they became too strong to be broken." "it is not too much in many cases to appraise early-formed habits as destiny."

普朗克："even in physics the radically new ideas are seldom really accepted by the old guard. Instead, said Planck, progress is made by a new generation that comes along, less brain-blocked by its previous conclusions." 爱因斯坦也未能幸免："an older Einstein never accepted the full implications of quantum mechanics."

被反改变模式保护的对象："one's previous conclusions, human loyalties, reputational identity, commitments, accepted role in a civilization, etc."

法庭反制："before making decisions, judges and juries are required to hear long and skillful presentations of evidence and arguments from the side they will not naturally favor... This helps prevent considerable bad thinking from first-conclusion bias. Similarly, other modern decision-makers will often force groups to consider skillful counterarguments before making decisions."

教育定义："Proper education is one long exercise in the augmentation of high cognition so that our wisdom becomes strong enough to destroy wrong thinking maintained by resistance to change."

链条警告："It is important not to thus put one's brain in chains before one has come anywhere near his full potentiality as a rational person."

爱/恨作为维持装置（倾向2+3）："it acts as a conditioning device that makes the liker or lover tend to 1) ignore the faults of, and comply with the wishes of, the object of his affection... 3) distort other facts to facilitate love."（恨为镜像：忽视所恨者的美德+扭曲事实facilitate仇恨）芒格处方："See it like it is and love anyway."（比富兰克林"婚前睁大眼婚后半闭眼"更硬）。德摩斯梯尼（倾向13）："What a man wishes, that also will he believe."

## 断言
大脑是单入口受精系统：第一结论进入后自动关门，且反改变模式不只保护结论，还保护忠诚/声誉身份/承诺/角色——所以"改主意"的成本被心理化地记为身份损毁而非信息更新。新思想的接受由两个通道完成：强行反训练（达尔文工作法：主动优先处理证伪证据）或代际更替（普朗克通道）。程序性强制（先听完反方再决）比意志力有效——法官模式是机构级解药。

## 投资迁移
- 持仓审计：每个重仓都问"这个观点是我卵里的第一颗精子吗"——第一结论锁的检验=能否写下比自己当初买入时更强的反方论（steel-counterargument-bar门槛）；写不出=锁死而非坚信。
- 对他人结论的折扣：管理层"一直这么说"的叙事、卖方维持多年的评级，按受精卵机制计价——改变他们的不是证据是退休。
- 机构设计：L4裁决模板强制先写空头最强论再写多头论（法官模式的项目化）；季度复盘先读证伪触发清单（thesis falsification先于narrative）。
- 爱/恨滤镜：爱股审计按"看清它本来的样子"标准重过一遍事实清单；敌意并购/竞争分析里对"讨厌的公司"的能力评估强制翻倍核查（见 past-success-attribution-audit）。
