## 论证
原文（Talk Three Q&A）："I did recently reverse the conclusions of two sets of engineers. How did I have enough confidence in such a complicated field to do that? Well... I saw huge reasons in the circumstances for bias in each set of engineers as each recommended a course of action very advantageous to itself. And what each was saying was so consonant with a natural bias that it made me distrust it. Also, perhaps I knew enough engineering to know that what they were saying didn't make sense. Finally, I found a third engineer who recommended a solution I approved. And thereafter, the second engineer came to me and said, 'Charlie, why didn't I think of that?' Which is to his credit. It was a much better solution, both safer and cheaper." 准则："You must have the confidence to override people with more credentials than you whose cognition is impaired by incentive-caused bias or some similar psychological force that is obviously present. But there are also cases where you have to recognize that you have no wisdom to add and that your best course is to trust some expert. In effect, you've got to know what you know and what you don't know. What could possibly be more useful in life than that?"

机制：专家的结论=专业知识×激励向量——当激励明显偏向自身（卖方研报、自家方案的工程师、承销商），结论的'过于合拍'本身就是证据；越过文凭的底气来自(a)偏见指纹的识别力(b)对该领域足够的常识(c)第三方交叉验证，三者缺一则退回'承认不懂、相信专家'。

迁移：读卖方研报/公司guidance的第一动作=标注作者激励向量（承销关系、持仓、奖金结构），'过于合拍'的推荐降权；L4裁决与市场共识冲突时的自信来源=对方激励审计+我方能力圈内；反向纪律：无(a)(b)(c)时对专家结论默认采信并声明——'我不知道'是合法输出（见bee-dance-antipattern）。
