## 论证
原文（Talk Ten, USC Gould法学院毕业演讲 2007-05-13）："the safest way to try to get what you want is to try to deserve what you want. It's such a simple idea. It's the golden rule. You want to deliver to the world what you would buy if you were on the other end... By and large, the people who have this ethos win in life, and they don't win just money and honors. They win the respect, the deserved trust of the people they deal with. And there is huge pleasure in life to be obtained from getting deserved trust." 葬礼测试："occasionally, you will find a perfect rogue of a person who dies rich and widely known. But mostly these people are fully understood as despicable by the surrounding civilization. If the cathedral is full of people at the funeral ceremony, most of them are there to celebrate the fact that the person is dead... And finally, one man came up and said, 'Well, his brother was worse.'" 无缝网："complex bureaucratic procedure does not represent the highest form civilization can reach. One higher form is a seamless, non-bureaucratic web of deserved trust. Not much fancy procedure, just totally reliable people correctly trusting one another. That's the way an operating room works at the Mayo Clinic. If lawyers would introduce a lot of lawyer-like process, more patients would die. So never forget, when you're a lawyer, that while you may have to sell procedure, you don't always have to buy. In your own life, what you want to maximize is a seamless web of deserved trust. And if your proposed marriage contract has 47 pages, my suggestion is that you not enter."

机制：'配得上'把目标函数从'获取'改写为'给付质量'——财富与荣誉成为副产品；deserved trust的定义性特征=可验证的履约历史（reliability的复利），程序只是信任缺失时的替代品，程序成本随信任降低而上升；葬礼测试是终局审计：声誉的现值在最后由'了解你的人'清算，不由营销清算。
迁移：审计公司文化核时的可操作判据——交易主要靠合同/审批流程还是靠deserved trust运转？流程越厚信任越薄（47页婚前协议定律）；个人职业目标从'升职'改写为'让了解我的人给我deserved trust'；D3文化核打分的行为级证据源之一=创始人是否炫耀'我打败了谁'（rogue信号）vs'我配得上谁的信任'。
