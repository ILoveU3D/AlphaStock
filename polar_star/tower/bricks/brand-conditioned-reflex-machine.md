## 论证
原文（Talk Four）："in essence, we are going into the business of creating and maintaining conditioned reflexes. The Coca-Cola trade name and trade dress will act as the stimuli, and the purchase and ingestion of our beverage will be the desired responses." 奖赏四类："Food value in calories or other inputs / Flavor, texture, and aroma... under neural pre-programming of man through Darwinian natural selection / Stimulus, as by sugar or caffeine / Cooling effect when man is too hot"——要lollapalooza结果就四类全要。可得性防御："We will make it a permanent obsession in our company that our beverage, as fast as practicable, will be at all times be available everywhere throughout the world. After all, a competing product, if it is never tried, can't act as a reward creating a conflicting habit. Every spouse knows that." 巴甫洛夫："the brain of man yearns for the type of beverage held by the pretty woman he can't have"; 名字与观感的选择（'Coca-Cola'而非'Glotz's Sugared, Caffeinated Water'、酒色、碳酸化、保密hoopla）全是巴甫洛夫设计。社会证明："imitative consumption triggered by mere sight of consumption, will not only help induce trial... it will also bolster perceived rewards"; "More than with most other products, increased selling power will come from each increase in sales." 定价架构："we will always set the first-sale price, either 1) for fountain syrup, or 2) for any container... make any independent bottler we need a subcontractor, not a vendee of syrup, and certainly not a vendee of syrup under a perpetual franchise specifying a syrup price frozen forever at its starting level."

机制：品牌不是'印象'的静态容器而是反射回路的运行时——刺激必须被反复购买兑现才维持条件反射，所以可得性缺位=反射消退；三通道同向是lollapalooza而非相加：巴甫洛夫给试饮以意义、社会证明放大每次销量、操作性奖赏锁习惯；定价架构是心理学的现金流翻译器——同一种反射，分销合同决定租金归谁。
迁移：消费特许审计五问：奖赏通道全不全（功能/感官/刺激/温度）？可得性是否'永远随处'？联想资产在与什么绑定？社会证明是否随销量自增强？分销/定价架构是否把灌装方变成分包商而不是永久固定价特许？任何一问答不上，反射机器有断点——DCF的分子就悬空；与brand-is-impression互补：段永平给纪律（无溢价谬误/时间不对称/谁改谁下台），芒格给机器图纸。
