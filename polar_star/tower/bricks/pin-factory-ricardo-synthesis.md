## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："Number one is Ricardo's principle of comparative advantage in trade, and the other is Adam Smith's pin factory... they're radically different examples in that one of them is the ultimate example of central planning—the pin factory—where the whole system was planned by somebody, while the other example, Ricardo's, happens automatically as a natural consequence of trade. And, of course, once you get into the joys of synthesis, you immediately think, 'Do these things interact?' Of course they interact. Beautifully. And that's one of the causes of the power of a modern economic system." 酒店案例："in came, in response to that sign, a man who said, 'I'll spend $200,000 fixing up your hotel and buy it at a high price on credit if you can get zoning so I can turn the parking lot into a putting green.'... my people are self-contained behind walls... they need a putting green... The odd system that this guy had designed to amuse seniors was pure pin factory, and finding the guy with this system was pure Ricardo." 教授："economics professors are most economical with ideas. They make a few they learned in graduate school last a lifetime." 十页测试："anybody with a high IQ who graduated in economics ought to be able to sit down and write a 10-page synthesis of all these ideas that's quite persuasive... They'd give me Ronald Coase. They'd talk about transaction costs. They'd click off a little something that their professors gave them and spit it back." 普朗克："Max Planck, the great Nobel laureate... tried once to do economics. He gave it up... 'It's too hard. The best solution you can get is messy and uncertain.'"

机制：比较优势=市场自发配置（看不见的手），别针厂=设计出的秩序（看得见的手）——现代经济不是二选一而是两者的乘积：系统设计者通过市场被发现（Ricardo），市场把资产交给最会设计系统的人（pin factory）；合成力稀缺的根源=学科训练奖励分解（考科斯是知识点，懂科斯为什么成立、与李嘉图如何咬合是合成）。
迁移：投资里'找运营者'重于'找资产'——困境资产的价值实现常需要换系统而非更努力执行旧系统（并购分析必问：这次交易把资产交到更擅长的人手里了吗）；芒格处理不良资产的笨办法（挂个牌子）胜过十英寸厚的咨询报告——搜索成本低于分析成本的领域用市场信号；对研究的启示：写'所有模型如何咬合'的合成文件比堆砌知识点值钱（model lint的'信息量≥年报'标准同源于此）。
