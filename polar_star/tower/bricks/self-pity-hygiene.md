## 论证
原文（Talk Ten）："Generally speaking, envy, resentment, revenge, and self-pity are disastrous modes of thought. Self-pity can get pretty close to paranoia. Paranoia is one of the very hardest things to reverse. You do not want to drift into self-pity. I had a friend who carried a thick stack of linen-based cards. When somebody would make a comment that reflected self-pity, he would slowly and portentously pull out his huge stack of cards, take the top one, and hand it to the person. The card said, 'Your story has touched my heart. Never have I heard of anyone with as many misfortunes as you.' Well, you can say that's waggery, but I suggest it can be mental hygiene. Every time you find you're drifting into self-pity, whatever the cause, even if your child is dying of cancer, self-pity is not going to help. Just give yourself one of my friend's cards. Self-pity is always counterproductive. It's the wrong way to think. And when you avoid it, you get a great advantage over everybody else, or almost everybody else, because self-pity is a standard response. And you can train yourself out of it."

机制：四毒的共同结构=把决策权让渡给一个'受害者账户'——妒忌让他人账户决定我的价格，怨恨/报复让过去账户决定现在，自怜让受害感决定行动；自怜→妄想的通路=每次自怜都在给'世界对我有阴谋'的模型加证据权重；'标准反应'意味着不修自怜的人把竞争优势白送给修掉它的人（负向alpha是可收割的）。
迁移：回撤复盘的自检第一问='我此刻在自怜吗'（drawdown归因前的情绪卫生前置）；亚麻卡片=审计journal时给自己的标准回信模板（幽默是除颤器的形式因——把叙事变成对象）；对四毒的检测在写作里最容易：出现'凭什么是我/他们就是坏'句式即触发；对企业文化的应用——受害者叙事浓的公司（'市场不懂我们/空头害我们'）在文化核扣分。
