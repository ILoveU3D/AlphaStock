## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："When technology moves as fast as it does in a civilization like ours, you get a phenomenon that I call competitive destruction... You know, you have the finest buggy whip factory, and all of a sudden, in comes this little horseless carriage." 早鸟优势："when these new businesses come in, there are huge advantages for the early birds. And when you're an early bird, there's a model that I call 'surfing'—when a surfer gets up and catches the wave and just stays there, he can go a long, long time. But if he gets off the wave, he becomes mired in shallows. But people get long runs when they're right on the edge of the wave, whether it's Microsoft or Intel or all kinds of people, including National Cash Register in the early days." Patterson收银机起源："he put it into his retail operation. And it instantly changed from losing money to earning a profit because it made it so much harder for the employees to steal... He thought, 'I'm going into the cash register business.'... He was a fanatic about everything important as the technology developed." 投资含义："a well-educated orangutan could see that buying into a partnership with Patterson in those early days, given his notions about the cash register business, was a total 100 percent cinch. And, of course, that's exactly what an investor should be looking for." IBM警示："There was enough turmoil in technological change that IBM got bounced off the wave after surfing successfully for 60 years."

机制：新技术打开的时间窗给最早的胜任者临时垄断经济（分销/专利/学习曲线全部先行锁定），浪的本质是有限——技术动荡会周期性掀翻骑浪者，60年的冲浪记录不是永续期的证据。

迁移：AI算力转型的候选审计=找'早年Patterson'（新范式+全要素狂热+分销/专利先行）；对骑浪者做DCF时终值显式折扣（浪还有多久的反身性问题）；'公司被抛下浪了吗'是三杀里'杀估值体系'的技术变体判据；巴菲特不骑科技浪不是浪不存在而是读不懂浪（见circle-of-competence）。
