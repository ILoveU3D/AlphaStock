## 论证
原文（Talk Ten）："maximizing non-egality will often work wonders. What do I mean? Well, John Wooden of UCLA presented an instructive example when he was the number one basketball coach in the world. He said to the bottom five players, 'You don't get to play; you are practice partners.' The top seven did almost all the playing. Well, the top seven learned more—remember the importance of the learning machine—because they were doing all the playing. And when he adopted that non-egalitarian system, Wooden won more games than he had won before. I think the game of competitive life often requires maximizing the experience of the people who have the most aptitude and the most determination as learning machines. And if you want the very highest reaches of human achievement, that's where you have to go. You do not want to choose a brain surgeon for your child by drawing straws to select one of 50 applicants, all of whom take turns doing procedures. You don't want your airplanes designed in too egalitarian a fashion. You don't want your Berkshire Hathaways run that way either. You want to provide a lot of playing time for your best players."

机制：上场时间=经验流量的分配，而经验是学习机器的燃料——平均分配经验等于平均稀释学习速度；与复利耦合后马太效应是算术结果不是道德选择（强者的经验增量滚进能力，能力再吸引更高价值的上场机会）；'抽签选脑外科医生'反例标出平等主义的适用边界——机会平等（入口）与结果平等（分配）必须分层处理。
迁移：公司文化核审计问题='关键决策/关键客户/关键项目是按资历轮换还是按能力集中？'——轮换型组织用公平感稀释了复利；投资组合同理：仓位集中给理解最深的机会（与concentration-by-understanding互证）；个人层=把每天的'上场时间'（精力最贵的时段）预留给最重要的事而不是平均分给日程表；防误用条款：非平等主义只适用于经验分配，不适用于人的尊严（对练伙伴也是系统的一部分）。
