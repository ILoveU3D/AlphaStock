## 论证
原文（Talk One 1986）："First, be unreliable. Do not faithfully do what you have engaged to do. If you will only master this one habit, you will more than counterbalance the combined effect of all your virtues, howsoever great... you will be outrun by hordes and hordes of mediocre turtles, and even some mediocre turtles on crutches." 反例："I had a roommate in college who was and is severely dyslexic. But he is perhaps the most reliable man I have ever known. He has had a wonderful life so far: an outstanding wife and children, chief executive of a multi-billion-dollar corporation." Talk One Revisited (2006)："I now believe even more strongly that 1) reliability is essential for progress in life, and 2) while quantum mechanics is unlearnable for a vast majority, reliability can be learned to great advantage by almost anyone." 及麦当劳："successfully taught most of them the one lesson they most need: to show up reliably for responsible work."

机制：信任是复利的载体（compounding 里'信任皆可指数增长'），而可靠是信任的唯一输入——不可靠把每次合作变成一次性博弈，等于对信任账户做永久性提款。'乌龟跑赢兔子成群'的比喻点明：可靠优势不依赖聪明，正因如此它是可普及的复利杠杆。

迁移：选合伙人/基金经理/公司管理层，可靠性的权重应高于智力（智力不可靠=高配破坏力）；自己养成'说到即做到'的最小闭环（承诺-兑现循环）是成本最低的人生复利启动器。
