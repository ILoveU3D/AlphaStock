## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："Many of you probably don't remember what happened after the South Sea Bubble, which caused an enormous financial contraction and a lot of pain. Except in certain rare cases, they banned publicly traded stock in England for decades. Parliament passed a law that said you can have a partnership with a few partners but you can't have publicly traded stock. And by the way, England continued to grow without publicly traded stock... Just as in real estate—we had all the shopping centers and auto dealerships and so on we needed for years when we didn't have publicly traded real estate shares. It's a myth that once you've got some capital market, economic considerations demand that it has to be as fast and efficient as a casino. It doesn't. And the people who are in the business of prospering because there's a lot of stock being traded in casino-like frenzy wouldn't like this example if they studied it enough."

机制：资本市场对实体经济的真实功能=资本形成与价格发现，两者都不需要秒级流动性与高频换手——中小企业融资靠银行/私募/合伙制即可运转；泡沫后的立法者用'关闭公开市场'止痛：代价是流动性收益，收益是狂热抑制，历史证明这个交换没有摧毁增长；本案例摧毁的是'市场结构必然性'叙事——当前的市场形态是选择的结果不是物理定律。
迁移：对'流动性=价值'的怀疑条款——流动性是持有人的便利、交易者的狂欢，不进入企业现金流（DCF无流动性项）；监管冲击叙事（T+1、印花税上调、减持新规）引发'市场完了'的恐慌时，先对照南海案例再评估真实影响；对换手率奇高/博彩化的板块按赌场税逻辑降权（croupier-tax-arithmetic的近邻）；永不需要为了'让市场满意'而牺牲企业资本的配置效率。
