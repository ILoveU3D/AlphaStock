## 论证
原文（Talk Three）："you need them in a form whereby, if there are 20 constructs, you have all 20. In other words, you shouldn't be operating with 10. And you need to use them as a checklist." 飞行员："No pilot takes off without going through his checklist: A, B, C, D… And no bridge player who needs two extra tricks plays a hand without going down his checklist... But these psychology professors think they're so smart that they don't need a checklist. But they aren't that smart. Almost nobody is. Or maybe nobody is. If they used a checklist, they'd realize the Milgram experiment harnesses six psychological principles at least—not three." 教材daft："just open a psychology text, turn to the index, and look up envy... It's just that psychology professors don't know about envy. Books that thick are teaching a psychology course without envy? And with no simple psychological denial? And no incentive-caused bias?" 伦理借口："Most professors solve this problem, in effect, by assuming 'If I can't demonstrate it with my experiments, then it doesn't exist.' However, obviously, that's asinine... you have to do the best you can with it, with such evidence as is available." 电磁学类比："Psychology, to me, as currently organized, is like electromagnetism after Faraday but before Maxwell—a lot has been discovered, but no one mind has put it all together in proper form."

机制：单个误判原理的预测力弱（人能部分抵消单因素），但多原理同向共振时预测力质变——所以必须全量携带否则共振不可见；清单对抗的正是聪明人的过度自信（'我认为我不需要'本身就是误判）。

迁移：L2红旗清单（intel红/借钱分红/利润暴增/周期陷阱）就是本条的机构化——扩展方向是把Milgram式'至少六条同向'审计做进深评模板；写作时自查：本分析里我漏了哪条误判原理（嫉妒/否认/激励偏见常缺）；'不可实验≠不存在'对无法完美验证的DCF终值、文化评估同样适用——证据可得范围内做到最好+声明缺口。
