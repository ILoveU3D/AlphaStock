## 论证
原文（Talk Four）："after our trademarked flavor dominates our new market, we must avoid making any huge and sudden change in our flavor. Even if a new flavor performs better in blind taste tests, changing to that new flavor would be a foolish thing to do... our old flavor will be so entrenched in consumer preference by psychological effects that a big flavor change would do us little good, and it would do immense harm by triggering in consumers the standard deprival super-reaction syndrome that makes 'take-aways' so hard to get in any type of negotiation and helps make most gamblers so irrational. Moreover, such a large flavor change would allow a competitor, by copying our old flavor, to take advantage of both 1) the hostile consumer super-reaction to deprival and 2) the huge love of our original flavor created by our previous work." New Coke判词："the brilliant and effective executives who, surrounded by business school and law school graduates, have run the Coca-Cola Company with glorious success in recent years also did not understand elementary psychology well enough to predict and avoid the New Coke fiasco, which dangerously threatened their company."

机制：剥夺超级反应=损失1单位的价值被心理计为约2-3单位（对称于损失厌恶），且'被剥夺的'越是被条件反射长期供养的对象，反应越暴烈；盲测剥离了刺激（商标、联想、社会证明）所以测的是产品液本身——但消费者买的从来是全套反射，改液=拆刺激-反应配对=对全部已建立反射的剥夺；管理层的盲测逻辑恰是'看似科学'的毁灭路径。
迁移：审计红旗——管理层用'更优配方/新包装/升级'语言对待旗舰SKU；适用面远超饮料：核心产品改版、UI重设计、药企剂型变更、奢侈品牌年轻化；逆向应用：当竞争者的旗舰被其管理层'升级'时，是复制其旧配方收割其反射基数的经典窗口（芒格文中 explicitly 给对手的战术）；本条是misjudgment清单中剥夺反应在生意政策层的投影。
