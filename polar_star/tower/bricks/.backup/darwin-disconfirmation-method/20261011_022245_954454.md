## 论证
原文（Talk One）："Darwin's result was due in large measure to his working method... he always gave priority attention to evidence tending to disconfirm whatever cherished and hard-won theory he already had. In contrast, most people early achieve and later intensify a tendency to process new and disconfirming information so that any original conclusion remains intact." 及"The life of Darwin demonstrates how a turtle may outrun a hare, aided by extreme objectivity, which helps the objective person end up like the only player without a blindfold in a game of Pin the Tail on the Donkey." Darwin 资质平平："Charles Robert Darwin would have ranked near the middle of the Harvard School graduating class of 1986."

机制：证伪主义（falsificationism）说'理论必须可证伪'；达尔文工作法补上操作时序——不是被动接受证伪检验，而是主动给反证排队优先权。情感成本是真实的（'珍爱且来之不易'），所以能执行的人极少，执行者的超额收益来自多数人的系统性不执行。爱因斯坦的'自我批评'定义同源："the testing and destruction of his own well-loved ideas."

迁移：塔的证伪集巡逻应该是每日队列头而不是尾部（thesis 的 falsification 字段先于 narrative 字段被读）；写完砖先自己找反例再发布；reading 重读时先找'当时不喜欢的章节'。
