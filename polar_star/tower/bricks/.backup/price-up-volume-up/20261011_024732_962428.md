## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："Now tell me several instances when, if you want the physical volume to go up, the correct answer is to increase the price. And there's this long and ghastly pause... maybe one person in 50 could name one instance." Ballhaus案例："he realized that the reason this thing was selling so poorly, even though it was better than anybody else's product, was because it was priced lower. It made people think it was a low-quality gizmo. So he raised the price by 20 percent or so, and the volume went way up." 主答案："Suppose you raise that price and use the extra money to bribe the other guy's purchasing agent?... And, of course, there are a zillion, once you've made that mental jump. It's so simple." 基金业："You raise the commissions, which, of course, reduces the number of units of real investments delivered to the ultimate buyer... You're bribing the broker to betray his client and put the client's money into the high-commission product. This has worked to produce at least a trillion dollars of mutual fund sales." 自守："I don't think it's necessary to spend your life selling what you would never buy. Even though it's legal, I don't think it's a good idea. But you shouldn't accept all my notions, because you'll risk becoming unemployable. You shouldn't take my notions unless you're willing to risk being unemployable by all but a few."

机制：供求曲线是默认心智模型，但真实市场里价格同时是①质量信号（信息不对称下买家用价格反推品质，便宜=可疑）与②渠道弹药（分销环节的激励可用毛利武装）——两个机制都能翻转价格-数量符号；'50人答1人'证明合成稀缺不是知识缺口而是模型调用缺口：五十分之一的人会背供求曲线，五十分之一之外无人被教过'什么时候曲线反向'。
迁移：定价审计条款——便宜有时是销量毒药（信任品/医疗器械/奢侈品尤其，'质优价廉'在信任品市场是伪策略）；渠道分析必问——谁在抽成、抽成流向谁的激励（基金销售费/渠道返点/带金销售）；评估'降价换量'策略时先确认品类是否信号敏感；个人层=不推销自己不愿买的东西（声誉的本分约束）。
