## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："Many markets get down to two or three big competitors—or five or six. And in some of those markets, nobody makes any money to speak of. But in others, everybody does very well." 航空："the net amount of money that's been made by the shareholders of airlines since Kitty Hawk is now a negative figure—a substantial negative figure. Competition was so intense that, once it was unleashed by deregulation, it ravaged shareholder wealth in the airline business." 麦片："If you're some kind of a medium-grade cereal maker, you might make 15 percent on your capital. And if you're really good, you might make 40 percent. But why are cereals so profitable, despite the fact that it looks to me like they're competing like crazy with promotions, coupons, and everything else? I don't fully understand it." Kellogg假想："if I were Kellogg and I decided that I had to have 60 percent of the market, I think I could take most of the profit out of cereals. I'd ruin Kellogg in the process. But I think I could do it... In some businesses, the participants behave like a demented Kellogg. In other businesses, they don't. Unfortunately, I do not have a perfect model for predicting how that's going to happen." 瓶装："That must get down to the peculiarities of individual adjustment to market capitalism. I think you'd have to know the people involved to fully understand what was happening."

机制：行业租金的存续不止看集中度（HHI），更看参与者的目标函数——份额最大化者会把全行业利润让渡给客户；理性玩家被迫跟随（不跟就丢份额），所以单一疯子即可摧毁池塘；诚实的边界：芒格承认没有预测模型，必须落到对'人'的了解（与D3文化核/管理层审计直通）。

迁移：行业分析清单加一栏'主要玩家的目标函数'（份额导向vs利润导向——看CEO激励结构和历史行为，价格战史比集中度数字更预示未来）；周期陷阱(cycle_trap)的微观机制即此律：全行业产能/份额疯狂期=池塘死亡期；L3深评时'认识当事人'=读管理层行为史（语录之外要看定价行为）。
