## 论证
原文（Talk Ten）："there are dangers in it because it works so well. If you use it, you will frequently find when you're with some expert from another discipline—maybe even an expert who is your employer, with a vast ability to harm you—that you know more than he does about fitting his specialty to the problem at hand... That is a very dangerous position to be in. You can cause enormous offense by being right in a way that causes somebody else to lose face in his own discipline or hierarchy. I never found the perfect way to avoid harm from this serious problem. Even though I was a good poker player when I was young, I wasn't good enough at pretending when I thought I knew more than my supervisors did... So I gave a lot of offense." 合伙人教训："Your duty is to behave in such a way that the client thinks he's the smartest person in the room. If you have any energy or insight available after that, use it to make your senior partner look like the second-smartest person in the room. And only after you've satisfied those two obligations do you want your light to shine at all." 自选："I usually moved with the drift of my nature, and if some other people didn't like it, well, I didn't need to be adored by everybody... My advice to you is to be better than I was at keeping insights hidden."

机制：正确性造成冒犯的机制=面子/层级是对方的身份资产，你的正确在其账上是负债（去剥夺感）——信息优势在权力劣势下自动转化为敌意成本；芒格的对策不是'不正确'而是'承担冒犯成本+不需要被所有人爱戴'（用独立财富与声誉支付锋芒成本）——预算化冒犯，而不是零冒犯。
迁移：与被投公司/专家/上级沟通时的默认姿势=把洞见包装进对方的框架或以问题形式交付（问题即尊重）；'不需要被所有人爱戴'是塔上多数反共识断言的心理成本预算——本系统输出verdict-first的分析必然冒犯某些叙事持有者，预算而非回避；与misjudgment-checklist-doctrine的张力：清单主义要求看见并说出错误，藏锋纪律要求管理说的成本——解：对事直接对人温润，对系统说真话对个人留面子。
