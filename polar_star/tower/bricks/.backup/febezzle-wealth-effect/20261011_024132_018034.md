## 论证
原文（Talk Seven, 2000）bezzle溯源："The embezzler spends more because he has more income, and his employer spends as before because he doesn't know any of his assets are gone." Keynes对照："when the government prints another $20 bill and uses it to buy a pair of shoes, the shoemaker has another $20, and no one feels poorer." febezzle定义与基金会指控："If a foundation or other investor wastes 3 percent of assets per year in unnecessary, nonproductive investment costs in managing a strongly rising stock portfolio, it still feels richer, despite the waste, while the people getting the wasted 3 percent, febezzlers though they are, think they are virtuously earning income. The situation is functioning like undisclosed embezzlement without being self-limited. Indeed, the process can expand for a long while by feeding on itself." 基金研究："the typical mutual fund investor gained at 7.25 percent per year in a 15-year period when the average stock fund gained at 12.8 percent per year... the real performance lag for investors was over 5 percent of assets per year in addition to whatever percentage per year the mutual funds, after expenses, lagged behind stock market averages." 换手病理："folly in the constant removal of assets from lagging portfolio managers being forced to liquidate stockholdings, followed by the placement of removed assets with new investment managers that have high-pressure, asset-gaining hoses in their mouths and clients whose investment results will not be improved by the super-rapid injection of new funds." 2006极限态："no money at all, net of investment costs, is going out of all corporations to all corporate owners combined... a weird and disturbing combination of 1) a gambling casino imposing an unreasonably greedy take for the house, plus 2) a form of Ponzi-like scheme, similar to the market for expensive art... plus 3) a bubble of speculation that would eventually burst."

机制：febezzle的宏观魔力来自'无痛提取'——成本从净值里匀速扣走，没有任何人收到一张'你被拿走了3%'的账单，所以消费函数感知不到损失；牛市掩盖一切（17%毛回报下3%无人清算），熊市以双重收缩显形（财富效应反向+成本继续抽）；行为差距的来源是赎回-申购的时序错位（卖掉落后者迫使其贱卖、追入领先者的'高压吸资管'），投资者自己给自己加荷官。
迁移：个人账户的febezzle审计——把每年佣金/税费/换手摩擦/汇率点差加总除以年均净资产，>1%即红灯；'觉得更富'的消费升级若由账面浮盈驱动，记一笔febezzle心理负债；对基金/理财产品的年费+申购赎回费+隐性换手全部折算进荷官税率；宏观应用：牛市里全社会被febezzle抬高的消费在熊市会反向，日本十年（利率零+巨额赤字仍无法重启消费）是下行不对称的实证——'经济学家终会承认：长期化的股市下跌对可选消费的每美元下行压力大于上涨的上行推力'。
