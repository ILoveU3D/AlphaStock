## 论证
原文（Talk One）："I cannot here pass by a reference to a life described as 'wonderful so far' without reinforcing the 'so far' aspects of the human condition by repeating the remark of Croesus, once the richest king in the world. Later, in ignominious captivity, as he prepared to be burned alive, he said, 'Well now do I remember the words of the historian Solon: No man's life should be accounted a happy one until it is over.'"

机制：中段成绩含未结算敞口——杠杆、健康、信任、运气都还没到最后定价时点；'so far'是把敞口当收益的语法标记。终身视角改变中途最优策略：可以毁掉终局的收益（爆仓/失信/健康透支）在中途都该打折到零权重。

迁移：基金的中途年化不结算survivorship与路径风险；人生重大决策用'80岁回看'贴现；对'连续N年无亏损'的记录先问尾部敞口在哪。
