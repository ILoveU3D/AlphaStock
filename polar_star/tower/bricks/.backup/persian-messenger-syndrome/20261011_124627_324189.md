## 原文（Talk Eleven 倾向10）
波斯信使："Ancient Persians actually killed some messengers whose sole fault was that they brought home truthful bad news, say, of a battle lost. It was actually safer for the messenger to run away and hide instead of doing his job as a wiser boss would have wanted it done."

现代版（CBS）："CBS, in its late heyday, was famous for the occurrence of Persian messenger syndrome because chairman Paley was hostile to people who brought him bad news. The result was that Paley lived in a cocoon of unreality from which he made one bad deal after another, even exchanging a large share of CBS for a company that had to be liquidated shortly thereafter."

诉讼案："years ago, two major oil companies litigated in a Texas trial court over some ambiguity in an operating agreement covering one of the largest oil reservoirs in the Western hemisphere. My guess is that the cause of the trial was some general counsel's unwillingness to carry bad news to a strong-minded CEO."

职业危险："It is actually dangerous in many careers to be a carrier of unwelcome news. Union negotiators and employer representatives often know this, and it leads to many tragedies in labor relations. Sometimes lawyers, knowing their clients will hate them if they recommend an unwelcome but wise settlement, will carry on to disaster."

解药："The proper antidote... is to develop, through exercise of will, a habit of welcoming bad news. At Berkshire, there is a common injunction: 'Always tell us the bad news promptly. It's only the good news that can wait.' It also helps to be so wise and informed that people fear not telling you bad news because you are so likely to get it elsewhere."

## 断言
惩罚坏消息的信使不是管理风格问题，是组织认知系统的自毁行为：坏消息通路是系统唯一的现实反馈回路，杀信使=亲手掐断供氧管，决策者随即住进"非现实的茧房"（cocoon of unreality）并在茧房里连续做成坏交易。解药是双件套：制度化的欢迎坏消息（伯克希尔戒律）+决策者自身消息灵通到"隐瞒会被拆穿"——后者让说真话从献祭变成安全。

## 投资迁移
- 文化核审计（本分审计）的坏消息通路检验：管理层访谈问"上次有人当面反驳CEO是什么时候""上一次主动披露坏消息的高管后来怎样了"；年报措辞里坏消息的位置与篇幅（藏脚注 vs 致股东信开头）是行为级证据。
- 投资人自己的版本：持仓的坏消息（证伪触发、数据缺口、逻辑裂缝）必须排读取队列头部；心理上"讨厌给我带来亏损消息的分析/信号"=在给自己的组合装波斯过滤器。
- CEO画像红旗：强势CEO（strong-minded）+层级化汇报体系+无外部制衡=信使死亡率的三个共变量；对这类公司的一切内部数据默认打折。
- L2红旗清单（intel红/借钱分红/利润暴增）本质是外部信使通道——公司内部信使死绝时，市场公开信息是最后的坏消息来源，红旗权重不可因管理层魅力下调。
