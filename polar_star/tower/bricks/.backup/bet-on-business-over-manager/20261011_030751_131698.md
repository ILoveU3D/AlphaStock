## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："ideally—and we've done a lot of this—you get into a great business that also has a great manager, because management matters... So you do get an occasional opportunity to get into a wonderful business that's being run by a wonderful manager. And, of course, that's hog heaven day. If you don't load up when you get those opportunities, it's a big mistake." 例外人："Occasionally, you'll find a human being who's so talented that he can do things that ordinary skilled mortals can't. I would argue that Simon Marks... was such a man. Patterson was such a man at National Cash Register. And Sam Walton was such a man. These people do come along, and, in many cases, they're not all that hard to identify. If they've got a reasonable hand—with the fanaticism and intelligence and so on that these people generally bring to the party—then management can matter very much." 裁决句："However, averaged out, betting on the quality of a business is better than betting on the quality of management. In other words, if you have to choose one, bet on the business momentum, not the brilliance of the manager. But very rarely, you find a manager who's so good that you're wise to follow him into what looks like a mediocre business."

机制：好生意自带系统惯性（平庸经理人被系统带着走也能复利），坏生意自带逆向水流（天才也要先逆流消耗掉生意的衰减再谈创造）；可识别的例外人有共同指纹——过往实绩+狂热+聚焦，缺一不可，所以例外清单短而稳定。

迁移：三核权重设计——商业模式核对企业文化核的'创始人魅力'子项有优先权：好生意+可信文化>平庸生意+魅力领袖；'跟人走'的合法性条件：例外人指纹齐全+他自己重仓+生意未死（核心可切除见cancer-surgery-formula）；识别任务不难但要纪律：宁可错过'maybe天才'也不给叙事光环打折。
