## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："Over the long term, it's hard for a stock to earn a much better return than the business that underlies it earns. If the business earns 6 percent on capital over 40 years and you hold it for that 40 years, you're not going to make much different than a 6 percent return, even if you originally buy it at a huge discount. Conversely, if a business earns 18 percent on capital over 20 or 30 years, even if you pay an expensive-looking price, you'll end up with one hell of a result. So the trick is getting into better businesses." 盖革计数器："the world wised up, and those real obvious bargains disappeared... the Ben Graham followers responded by changing the calibration on their Geiger counters." 突破："we realized that some company that was selling at two or three times book value could still be a hell of a bargain because of momentums implicit in its position, sometimes combined with an unusual managerial skill plainly present." 钱的来源："the bulk of the billions in Berkshire Hathaway have come from the better businesses. Much of the first $200 or $300 million came from scrambling around with our Geiger counter. But the great bulk of the money has come from the great businesses." Mr. Market："Graham treated it as a manic-depressive who comes by every day... it was a blessing to be in business with a manic-depressive who gave you this series of options all the time." 反面警告："Since it's so obvious that investing in great companies works, it gets horribly overdone from time to time. In the 'nifty 50' days, everybody could tell which companies were the great ones. So they got up to 50, 60, and 70 times earnings... a large investment disaster resulted from too-high prices."

机制：入场折价是持有期内一次性兑现的价差，生意回报率是每年重复复利的速率——n年后复利项碾压价差项；但价格纪律不可废：好生意+50-70倍=nifty-50灾难，说明收敛律裁决的是'生意质量决定长期回报的上限'，价格决定你能不能拿到这个回报。

迁移：DCF第一输入=生意的再投资回报率×存续期（核心三之'机器生命周期'），价格是第二输入；'看起来贵的好生意vs明显便宜的中等生意'的裁决程序：先算前者18%型路径的兑现条件，再算后者6%型+折价的兑现条件；估值锚（三杀判据）必须建在生意回报上，nifty-50是杀价格层教训的实名案例。
