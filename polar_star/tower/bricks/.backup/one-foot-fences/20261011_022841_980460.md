## 论证
原文（Talk Three Q&A）："We're always looking for something where we think we have an insight that gives us a big statistical advantage... We have no system for having automatic good judgment on all investment decisions that can be made. Ours is a totally different system. We just look for no-brainer decisions. As Buffett and I say over and over again, we don't leap seven-foot fences. Instead, we look for one-foot fences with big rewards on the other side. So we've succeeded by making the world easy for ourselves, not by solving hard problems." 不可知论："98 percent of the time, our attitude toward the market is that we're agnostics. We don't know. Is GM valued properly vis-à-vis Ford? We don't know." 认得出才有用："It doesn't help us merely for favorable odds to exist. They have to be in a place where we can recognize them. So it takes a mispriced opportunity that we're smart enough to recognize. And that combination doesn't occur often. But it doesn't have to. If you wait for the big opportunity and have the courage and vigor to grasp it firmly when it arrives, how many do you need? For example, take the top 10 business investments Berkshire Hathaway's ever made. We would be very rich if we'd never done anything else—in two lifetimes."

机制：难点在于好生意容易看出来但价格常把'哪只股票最好'的问题变难——难度的来源是竞争（无数聪明人抢），所以正解是切换游戏：只在'生意好+价格错+我懂'三条件交集处下注；难度系数不计分（1994巴菲特），跨栏高度是自选的。

迁移：recommend的'唯一pick'标准应该是一英尺栏测试（三条件交集缺一即弃）；'GM vs Ford哪个好'式问题诚实回答不知道（对比报告的边界感）；本AI的自我期许=成为'认得出错价'的筛子而非全知分析师——报告里的'不知道'是交付物的一部分；宁可错过十个难判断的，不错过一个送分的。
