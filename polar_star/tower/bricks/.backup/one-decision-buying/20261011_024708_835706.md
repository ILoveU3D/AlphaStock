## 论证
原文（Ch3，芒格原话）："We're partial to putting out large amounts of money where we won't have to make another decision. If you buy something because it's undervalued, then you have to think about selling it when it approaches your calculation of its intrinsic value. That's hard. But if you can buy a few great companies, then you can sit on your ass. That's a good thing." 及 sit-on-your-ass investing 的收益结构："You're paying less to brokers, you're listening to less nonsense, and if it works, the tax system gives you an extra one, two, or three percentage points per annum."

机制：每次决策是新的犯错入口+摩擦成本+注意力占用。低估修复型策略的隐藏成本不是"错了"而是"对了以后还得再对一次"（卖点决策）——内含价值是区间不是点，逼近时的卖出决策精度远低于买入决策精度。伟大公司买入把持有期推向无限久期，决策频率趋零。

迁移：为什么 wonderful-at-fair 完胜 fair-at-wonderful 的决策经济学解释；对工具箱的映射=mid/long horizon 的推荐结构（-50%可承受+屁股坐穿）；对个人=人生大决策（伴侣/城市/职业主干）也应买"免再决策"的版本。
