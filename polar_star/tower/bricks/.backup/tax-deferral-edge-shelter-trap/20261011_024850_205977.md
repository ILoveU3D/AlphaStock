## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："If you're going to buy something that compounds for 30 years at 15 percent per annum and you pay one 35 percent tax at the very end... after taxes, you keep 13.3 percent per annum. In contrast, if you bought the same investment but had to pay taxes every year of 35 percent out of the 15 percent that you earned, then your return would be... only 9.75 percent per year compounded. So the difference there is over 3.5 percent." 一般化："Even with a 10 percent per annum investment, paying a 35 percent tax at the end gives you 8.3 percent after taxes... if you pay the 35 percent each year instead of at the end, your annual result goes down to 6.5 percent. So you add nearly 2 percent of after-tax return per annum." 坐出来的优势："If you sit on your ass for long, long stretches in great companies, you can get a huge edge from nothing but the way income taxes work. You're paying less to brokers. You're listening to less nonsense." 陷阱："trying to minimize taxes too much is one of the great standard causes of really dumb mistakes. I see terrible mistakes from people being overly motivated by tax considerations. Warren and I personally don't drill oil wells. We pay our taxes... Anytime somebody offers you a tax shelter from here on in life, my advice would be don't buy it. In fact, anytime anybody offers you anything with a big commission and a 200-page prospectus, don't buy it... over a lifetime, you'll be a long way ahead."

机制：递延把税从年年度量衡变成终值一次性成本，复利间隙不被每年抽血——1.5-3.5pp/年是持有期的函数，坐得越久红利越大；避税结构则相反：设计动机是卖方佣金，复杂度正是错误藏身处，两百年复利常识让位于税表格式。

迁移：长持复利机器天然带税差护城河——账户/工具选择（分红的税务处理、卖出节奏）优化到'不改变持有决策'为止；'税务优化'作为买入理由之一的标的一票否决（与借钱分红否决同列红旗）；红线合并成一句话：坐得住的税差是收益，追着买的避税是损失。
