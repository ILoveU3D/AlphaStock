## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："Envy wisely got a very strong condemnation in the laws of Moses... Those old Jews knew how envious people are and how much trouble it caused. They really laid it on hard, and they were right. But Mandeville—remember his fable of bees? He demonstrated convincingly, to me, anyway, that envy was a great driver of proclivity to spend. And so, here's this terrible vice, which is forbidden in the Ten Commandments, and here it's driving all these favorable results in economics. There's some paradox in economics that nobody's going to get out." 哥德尔："When I was young, everybody was excited by Gödel... they've come up with more irremovable defects in mathematics and have decided that you're never going to get mathematics without some paradox in it... the poor economists are never going to get rid of paradoxes, nor are any of the rest of us. It doesn't matter. Life is interesting with some paradox." 边缘信号："When I run into a paradox, I think either I'm a total horse's ass to have gotten to this point or I'm fruitfully near the edge of my discipline. It adds excitement to life to wonder which it is."

机制：妒忌的微观作用=地位消费驱动器（相对性偏好，凡勃伦机制前身）——它同时是破坏性情绪（十诫层）与系统燃料（消费引擎层），曼德维尔命题private vices, public benefits的实质：社会系统的产出不可分离地与其恶因耦合；哥德尔类比的实质=任何足够丰富的形式系统必有不可消除的内在张力，社会系统比数学系统更甚。
迁移：消费股分析的暗引擎清单加一条——产品是否激活妒忌/身份焦虑=需求可持续性的心理来源（但文化核审计时按本分观照妖：靠妒忌生长的生意在'恶'字上记账，文化分不给）；碰壁启发式用于研究纪律——撞上反例/张力先标记不删除（塔的tension机制即此条的制度化）；对自己'消除全部矛盾'的冲动保持警惕，有些张力是系统的结构属性。
