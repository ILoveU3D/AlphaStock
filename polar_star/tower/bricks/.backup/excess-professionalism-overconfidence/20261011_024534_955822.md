## 论证
原文（Talk Six）："an excess of what seems like professionalism will often hurt you horribly, precisely because the careful procedures themselves often lead to overconfidence in their outcome." GM案："Using fancy consumer surveys—its excess of professionalism—it concluded not to put a fourth door in a truck designed to serve as the equivalent of a comfortable five-passenger car. Its competitors, more basic, had actually seen five people enter and exit cars... biological creatures ordinarily prefer effort minimization in routine activities and don't like removals of long-enjoyed benefits. There are only two words that come instantly to mind... and one of those words is 'oops.'" LTCM案："Smart, hardworking people aren't exempted from professional disasters from overconfidence. Often, they just go aground in the more difficult voyages they choose, relying on their self-appraisals that they have superior talents and methods." 副作用律："most good things have undesired side effects, and thinking is no exception." 防御："The best defense is that of the best physicists, who systematically criticize themselves to an extreme degree."

机制：流程产生两个毒——①流程的仪式感被误当证据的替代品（survey做了≠结论对）；②聪明人把流程强度误译为方法优越，于是选更难的题（杠杆、复杂性），失败期望随难度上升而智力不提供豁免；基本观察（人怎么用门）与花哨流程的信息优劣是倒挂的：前者直接命中因果，后者隔着一层方法学。
迁移：审计红旗——研究报告的方法学篇幅与结论篇幅之比过大=专业主义过剩嫌疑；自己的DCF也一样：假设表越精密越要警惕'流程自信'，lint标准是信息量≥年报而非模板字段填满；LTCM判词适用于一切杠杆+高IQ组合（含个人加杠杆炒股）；芒格开方子：流程之后强制加一个Feynman步骤——'我最容易在哪里骗了自己'，写出来再下结论。
