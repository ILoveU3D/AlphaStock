## 论证
原文（Talk Two）：advantages of scale "are ungodly important... which businesses succeed and which businesses fail"。六通道原文见statement引文；窄播反杀："narrower specialization... They beat our brains out as we relied on our broader magazine... its profit margins would make you salivate. So occasionally, scaling down and intensifying gives you the big advantage. Bigger is not always better." 官僚诅咒："as you get big, you get the bureaucracy. And with the bureaucracy comes the territoriality, which is again grounded in human nature... you think the work is done when it goes out of your in-basket into somebody else's in-basket. But, of course, it isn't... big, fat, dumb, unmotivated bureaucracies... 'If you won't bother me, I won't bother you, and we're both happy.'" GE例外："That's because they have a combination of a genius and a fanatic running it. And they put him in young enough, so he gets a long run." 连锁店："huge purchasing power... a whole bunch of little laboratories... And you get specialization." 盐笑话（"You must sell a lot of salt." "No. But you should see the guy who sells me salt."）。沃尔玛："He invented practically nothing. But he copied everything anybody else ever did that was smart—and he did it with more fanaticism and better employee manipulation." 拳击战略："He went out and fought 42 palookas... Then, as he got bigger, he started destroying the big boys." 总结："life is an everlasting battle between those two forces: to get these advantages of scale on one side and a tendency to get a lot like the US Department of Agriculture on the other side."

机制：规模的收益走外部市场通道（成本/心智/分销），规模的成本走内部代理通道（地盘/共谋/钝化）——两线异步增长，净优势=外增益−内损耗，企业文化决定内损耗系数；小对手的窄播是生态位策略：放弃规模通道、专吃大公司覆盖不到的深度。

迁移：审计大公司=逐通道检查规模优势还在扩还是见顶+官僚病哪个器官恶化（年报里找'出筐即完成'语言）；对'大公司不可战胜'的叙事先查官僚损耗；小公司研究标的的窄播利润率是护城河的合法形态；Walton式'复制最优+狂热执行'是弱者正面战。
