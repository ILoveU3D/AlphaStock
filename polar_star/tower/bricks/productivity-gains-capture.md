## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："The great lesson in microeconomics is to discriminate between when technology is going to help you and when it's going to kill you. And most people do not get this straight in their heads. But a fellow like Buffett does." 纺织织机："the people came to Warren and said, 'They've invented a new loom that we think will do twice as much work as our old ones.' And Warren said, 'Gee, I hope this doesn't work—because if it does, I'm going to close the mill.' And he meant it." 归属："the huge productivity increases that would come from a better machine introduced into the production of a commodity product would all go to the benefit of the buyers of the textiles. Nothing was going to stick to our ribs as owners." 反面（Oshkosh唯一报纸）："all of the savings would come right through to the bottom line." 第二步："they don't do the second step of the analysis, which is to determine how much is going to stay home and how much is just going to flow through to the customer. I've never seen a single projection incorporating that second step in my life... 'This capital outlay will save you so much money that it will pay for itself in three years.'... And after 20 years of doing it, somehow you've earned a return of only about 4 percent per annum. That's the textile business."

机制：节约的分配由需求价格弹性×竞争烈度决定——商品（无差异化+产能过剩）里客户知道你省了钱就会压价直到节约全额让渡；特许（差异化+局部垄断）里客户无从压价，节约归股东；设备商的激励是成交所以只演示第一步，系统性偏差因此进入一切资本预算。

迁移：评估公司AI/自动化/降本叙事的必答题=第二步：这生意是价格接受者还是制定者（对应franchise-moat三特征）；D3商业模式核的机构化问题——'节约会不会stick to our ribs'；对商品生意的高capex计划直接按'客户得利'折价其DCF贡献；科技股分析里'技术领先'本身是中性词，归属判定后才分敌我。
