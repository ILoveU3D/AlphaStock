## 论证
原文（Talk Nine, UCSB 2003）："My fourth criticism is that there's too much emphasis on macroeconomics and not enough on microeconomics. I think this is wrong. It's like trying to master medicine without knowing anatomy and chemistry. Also, the discipline of microeconomics is a lot of fun. It helps you correctly understand macroeconomics, and it's a perfect circus to do. In contrast, I don't think macroeconomics people have all that much fun. For one thing, they are often wrong because of extreme complexity in the system they wish to understand." 家具店："Is this a low-priced store or a high-priced store?... And what does a big store do? It provides a big selection. So what could this possibly be except a low-priced store with a big selection?... It costs a fortune to open a store this big, so nobody's done it before." Les Schwab："he must have harnessed what Mankiw calls the superpower of incentives... And he must be pretty good at advertising. Which he is. He's an artist... a talented fanatic had to get a hell of a lot of things right and keep them right with clever systems." 商学院判词："We hire business school graduates, and they're no better at these problems than you were. Maybe that's the reason we hire so few of them."

机制：宏观的复杂性来自互动项的指数爆炸（同场他名言：'后果有后果，经济学让气象学看起来像茶会'），所以宏观预测=对不可预测系统做外推，正确率注定低；微观是局部均衡：变量少、可验证、有套利闭环，'几个基本概念就能像热刀切黄油一样解开看起来很难的微观问题'——而且'这种思维方式报酬极高'。
迁移：投资里公司层（微观）优先于行业配置与大盘择时（宏观）——本工具ask单公司视图先于overview、L1池按公司而非板块构建；宏观叙事（利率/汇率/周期）只能做否决条件不能做买入理由（与data-cannot-buy同构）；对'宏观大师'型研究员的产出先打对折。
