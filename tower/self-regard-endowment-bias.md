## 原文（Talk Eleven 倾向12）
普遍高估："He mostly misappraises himself on the high side, like the 90 percent of Swedish drivers who judge themselves to be above average. Such misappraisals also apply to a person's major 'possessions.' One spouse usually over-appraises the other spouse. And a man's children are likewise appraised higher by him..."

禀赋效应："Once owned, they suddenly become worth more to him than he would pay if they were offered for sale to him and he didn't already own them. There is a name in psychology for this over-appraise-your-own-possessions phenomenon: the endowment effect. All man's decisions are suddenly regarded by him as better than would have been the case just before he made them."

自选号码："In lotteries, the play is much lower when numbers are distributed randomly than when the player picks his own number. This is quite irrational. The odds are almost exactly the same and much against the player."

面签陷阱："Excesses of self-regard often cause bad hiring decisions because employers grossly over-appraise the worth of their own conclusions that rely on impressions in face-to-face contact. The correct antidote to this sort of folly is to underweigh face-to-face impressions and overweigh the applicant's past record." 惠普案："a job candidate who is a marvelous presenter often causes great danger under modern executive search practice... Hewlett-Packard made a bad decision when it chose Ms. Fiorina, and this bad decision would not have been made if Hewlett-Packard had taken the methodological precautions it would have taken if it knew more psychology."

托尔斯泰效应："the worst criminals don't appraise themselves as all that bad. They come to believe either that 1) they didn't commit their crimes, or 2) considering the pressures and disadvantages of their lives, it is understandable and forgivable that they behaved as they did..." "Fixable but unfixed bad performance is bad character and tends to create more of itself, causing more damage to the excuse-giver with each tolerated instance, and 2) in demanding places, like athletic teams and General Electric, you are almost sure to be discarded in due course if you keep giving excuses instead of behaving as you should." 制度解药："a fair, meritocratic, demanding culture... plus severance of the worst offenders."

偷糖故事："Son, it would be better for you to simply take all you want and call yourself a thief every time you do it."

反毒剂总则："force yourself to be more objective when you are thinking about yourself, your family and friends, your property, and the value of your past and future activity."

骄傲的正面（诚实条款）："self-regard in the form of a justified pride in a job well done, or a life well lived, is a large constructive force. Without such justified pride, many more airplanes would crash." "perhaps the most desirable is a justified pride in being trustworthy." "Never underestimate the man who overestimates himself."

## 断言
自视过高不是单一偏差而是估值系统性漂移：对自己的能力、配偶、子女、已拥有之物、已做出的决定全部高估，且禀赋效应让"拥有"本身产生估值（对分析师：已建仓/已写报告的观点自动升值）。面签印象是重灾区——参与感（face-to-face involvement）被记成证据质量。托尔斯泰效应把个人版升级为组织版：为可修复的差绩效找借口而不修复=坏品格的自我复制器。

## 投资迁移
- 面签权重纪律直接移植到管理层评估：路演/访谈印象降权，过往经营记录（资本配置史、承诺兑现史）升权——marvelous presenter型CEO是数据高危项，Fiorina判词适用。
- 禀赋效应的持仓版：已持有=已拥有，卖出评估必须用"今天空仓会买吗"的置换检验剥离禀赋溢价；对自己写的thesis在复核时按"别人写的"标准重审。
- 托尔斯泰效应的持仓审计：差业绩+管理层解释（行业周期/一次性因素/宏观）连续两个季度出现=借口模式启动，按"可修而不修是坏品格"计价。
- 组织识别：找借口文化 vs meritocratic demanding culture（敢开除最差者）是文化核的分水岭证据；伯克希尔式"42年零CEO跳槽"背后是要求苛刻+公平的文化。
