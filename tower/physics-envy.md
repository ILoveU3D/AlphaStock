## 论证
原文（Ch3）："he is careful to avoid what he calls physics envy, the common human craving to reduce enormously complex systems (such as those in economics) to one-size-fits-all Newtonian formulas. Instead, he faithfully honors Albert Einstein's admonition: 'A scientific theory should be as simple as possible, but no simpler.' Or, in his own words: 'What I'm against is being very confident and feeling that you know, for sure, that your particular action will do more good than harm. You're dealing with highly complex systems wherein everything is interacting with everything else.'"

机制：物理学的公式成功依赖可重复实验+封闭系统+参数可测；经济系统是开放、适应、反身（参与者会改变系统本身）的——公式崇拜的本质是想借用物理学的确定性声望而非其方法论。虚假精确的具体危害=给人"已解决"的错觉，从而移除本应保留的安全边际。

迁移：对一切带小数点的预测先问'这配得上几个有效数字'（毛估估律的具体化）；黑箱模型输出不是估值是估值姿态；DCF的正确用法=倒推市场预期（reverse-DCF）而非假装算出真值——两处应用都已在工具箱落地。
