## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："Obviously, I was using a simple search engine in my mind to go through checklist-style, and I was using some rough algorithms that work pretty well in a great many complex systems... Extreme success is likely to be caused by some combination of the following factors: Extreme maximization or minimization of one or two variables. Example, Costco or our furniture and appliance store. / Adding success factors so that a bigger combination drives success, often in nonlinear fashion, as one is reminded by the concept of breakpoint and the concept of critical mass in physics. Often results are not linear. You get a little bit more mass and you get a lollapalooza result. And of course, I've been searching for lollapalooza results all my life, so I'm very interested in models that explain their occurrence. / An extreme of good performance over many factors. Example, Toyota or Les Schwab. / Catching and riding some sort of big wave. Example, Oracle." 双向："I recommend and use in problem-solving cut-to-the-quick algorithms, and I find you have to use them both forward and backward." 跳棋谜题："It can't be anything requiring a lot of hand-eye coordination. Nobody 85 years of age is going to win a national billiards tournament... Then he figured it couldn't be chess... too hard. The complexity of the system and the stamina required are too great. But that led into checkers. And he thought, 'Aha! There's a game where vast experience might guide you to be the best even though you're 85 years of age.'"

机制：四因子是lollapalooza的成因学分解——单因子极限化造护城河（模仿者面对全组织重构成本）；多因子同向叠加触发临界质量（线性叠加在断点处跳变为非线性爆发）；全因子优异是复利式积累（丰田式持续改善）；大浪是时代β（浪退后剩多少决定终值）；'正向'用于归因已成功的公司，'反向'用于排除法筛选（先把不可能的划掉）。
迁移：审计任何极端成功公司先归因到具体因子：它的成功是④浪吗？浪退了剩什么（①③的残余）？候选公司'想成功'时反问缺哪个因子、缺的能不能买/建；D4短线模式的周K上行结构检测本质是因子④的机器化；本工具红旗清单的'先否决'正是反向使用——排除比确认可靠（同场：'我不见很多灯泡亮起来'，说明清单思维稀缺即是套利空间）。
