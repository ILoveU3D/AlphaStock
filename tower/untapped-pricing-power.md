## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："There are actually businesses that you will find a few times in a lifetime where any manager could raise the return enormously just by raising prices, and yet they haven't done it. So they have huge untapped pricing power that they're not using. That is the ultimate no-brainer." 迪士尼："It's such a unique experience to take your grandchild to Disneyland... Disney found that it could raise those prices a lot and the attendance stayed right up. So a lot of the great record of Eisner and Wells was utter brilliance, but the rest came from just raising prices at Disneyland and Disneyworld and through videocassette sales of classic animated movies." See's/可乐："Warren and I raised the prices of See's Candies a little faster than others might have. And, of course, we invested in Coca-Cola, which had some untapped pricing power. And it also had brilliant management. So a Goizueta and Keough could do much more than raise prices. It was perfect." 捡钱："There are actually people out there who don't price everything as high as the market will easily stand. And once you figure that out, it's like finding money in the street, if you have the courage of your convictions."

机制：提价权未被使用而持久存在，说明护城河深于管理层自己的认知（竞争和客户本该惩罚提价却没惩罚）；'任何管理层都能做'意味着不需要天才假设——估值里把它当作免费期权，兑现即现金流上调，不兑现也不损失。

迁移：L3商业模式核的显式检查项=提价测试（过去十年价格轨迹vs通胀vs销量：敢提且量不减=护城河实证）；找到未动用提价权时把它加进DCF的现金流路径而非贴现率魔法；反向警示：连续激进提价而量跌=提价权正在透支（franchise三特征的证伪信号）。
