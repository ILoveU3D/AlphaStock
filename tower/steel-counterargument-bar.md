## 论证
原文（Talk Ten）："I have what I call an iron prescription that helps me keep sane when I drift toward preferring one intense ideology over another. I feel that I'm not entitled to have an opinion unless I can state the arguments against my position better than the people who are in opposition. I think that I am qualified to speak only when I've reached that state. This sounds almost as extreme as the iron prescription Dean Acheson was fond of attributing to William the Silent of Orange, who roughly said, 'It's not necessary to hope in order to persevere.' That probably is too tough for most people, although I hope it won't ever become too tough for me. My way of avoiding over-intensity in ideology is easier than Acheson's injunction and worth learning. This business of not drifting into extreme ideology is very, very important in life. If you want to end up wise, heavy ideology is very likely to prevent that outcome."

机制：'说得比对方更好'是最强形式的steelman——它强制认知系统里同时持有正反两个模型的满血版本，此时持有观点=压缩后的加权结论而非阵营身份；与第一结论偏见互斥：无法通过该门槛的'观点'自动降级为'倾向'（可以有倾向性怀疑，不可有神圣化确信——ideology-distorts-cognition的操作化）。
迁移：本塔对一切law级断言的入塔门槛=写brick前先写反方最强论（Field Notes里已有证伪条款传统）；投资判断的发言资格自查='我能把空头论点写得让空头点头吗'——不能就还没资格重仓；对AI输出的审计同样适用：让模型先argue against自身推荐再裁决（two-track-analysis的辩护人结构同源）；'不必希望也能坚持'是对抗结果导向焦虑的操作纪律。
