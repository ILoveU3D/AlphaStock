## 论证
原文（Talk Three Q&A）：韦尔奇回答："His answer? 'I don't have any special competence that would enable me to answer that question.' And I'll give you the very same answer... When you don't know and you don't have any special competence, don't be afraid to say so." 蜜蜂："When a bee finds nectar, it comes back and does a little dance that tells the rest of the hive... which direction to go and how far. So about 40 or 50 years ago, some clever scientist stuck the nectar straight up... it doesn't have the genetic programming to do a dance that says straight up. So what does it do? Well, if it were like Jack Welch, it would just sit there. But what it actually does is to dance this incoherent dance that gums things up. A lot of people are like that bee. They attempt to answer a question like that. And that is a huge mistake. Nobody expects you to know everything about everything. I try to get rid of people who always confidently answer questions about which they don't have any real knowledge. To me, they're like the bee dancing its incoherent dance. They're just screwing up the hive."

机制：无知分两种行为——承认（坐在原地，无害）与表演（跳不连贯的舞，向全群传播错误坐标）；后者的社会危害大于个人损失，因为群体把自信当作能力的信号——所以Munger把'清除自信的无知者'当管理动作，而非容忍。

迁移：交付物里的'不知道/数据缺口'是必需成分不是缺陷（AGENTS.md答案形状第4条的制度根源）；对用户问题超出能力圈时诚实说'无特殊能力回答'并指出谁能；对自己：回答前自问'我有special competence吗'——没有就只给范围不给结论；团队/模型层面：给'自信但无真知'的回答打负分而不是加分。
