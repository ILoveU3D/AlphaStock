## 论证
原文（Talk Three）："A very significant fraction of the people in the world will steal if a) it's very easy to do and b) there's practically no chance of being caught. And once they start stealing, the consistency principle... will soon combine with operant conditioning to make stealing habitual. So if you run a business where it's easy to steal because of your methods, you're working a great moral injury on the people who work for you." 机制叠加："if somebody else does it, now you've got at least two psychological principles: incentive-caused bias plus social proof. Not only that but you get Serpico effects: If enough people are profiting in a general social climate of doing wrong, then they'll turn on you and become dangerous enemies if you try and blow the whistle." 工伤案例："his premiums reached double-digit percentages of payroll... he closed his plant and moved the work to Utah among a community of believing Mormons... his workers' compensation expense is today? It's 2 percent of payroll." 设计律："it's much better to let some things go uncompensated—to let life be hard—than to create systems that are easy to cheat... You must stop slop early. It's very hard to stop slop and moral failure if you let it run for a while." 道德边界："Not all of what you know how to do should you use to manipulate people. Also, if you're willing to transcend the moral limits and the person you're trying to manipulate realizes what you're doing because he also understands the psychology, he'll hate you." Cook酸菜（正用）："Officers ate at one place where the men could observe them. And for a long time, he served sauerkraut to the officers but not to the men. And then, finally, Captain Cook said, 'Well, the men can have it one day a week.'... I regard that as a very constructive use of elementary psychology."

机制：人的诚实是情境函数不是人格常量——制度决定'容易+不被抓'的供给端，作弊的边际成本由系统设计者设定；作弊的复利性质（习惯化+社会证明扩散+Serpico自卫）让放任的代价非线性增长，所以止损的时间价值极高（趁早）。

迁移：审计公司内控=找'容易+不被抓'的缺口（关联交易、渠道压货、费用科目）而非只看账面；并购尽调问题=目标公司里哪些slop已经跑了很久（文化坏疽几乎不可逆）；政府补贴/牌照类生意的'容易作弊'结构是估值折扣项；自身系统（交易纪律、复盘）同样适用——规则要设计成作弊困难，而不是靠意志力。
