## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："I've had many friends in the sick-business-fix game over a long lifetime. And they practically all use the following formula—I call it the cancer surgery formula. They look at this mess, and they figure out if there's anything sound left that can live on its own if they cut away everything else. And if they find anything sound, they just cut away everything else. Of course, if that doesn't work, they liquidate the business. But it frequently does work." GEICO："GEICO had a perfectly magnificent business—submerged in a mess, but still working. Misled by success, GEICO had done some foolish things... All they had to do was to cut out all the folly and go back to the perfectly wonderful business that was lying there... think of all the money we passively made. It was a wonderful business combined with a bunch of foolishness that could easily be cut out. And people were coming in who were temperamentally and intellectually designed so they were going to cut it out." 频率："you may find one or two or three in a long lifetime that are very good. And you may find 20 or 30 that are good enough to be quite useful."

机制：坏成绩的来源可分离——可切除的愚蠢（错配的扩张、错误定价、代理成本）与核心已死（需求范式迁移）在DCF上完全不同：前者核心的独立现金流完好，愚蠢只是定期扣减项，切除即修复；手术执行者本身的气质是公式的一部分，因为切除是暴力动作，官僚做不了。

迁移：错杀车道（lane A）复核的分流问题='坏成绩来自可切除的愚蠢还是核心已死'（后者=三杀第三层，任何价格都贵）；管理层审计问题='新来的人是天生切除者还是官僚'（看其过往手术史）；turnaround标的的DCF=核心独立估值−愚蠢存量成本−手术摩擦，不给'转型叙事'打协同折扣。
