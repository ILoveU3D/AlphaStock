## 论证
原文（Talk Ten）："the acquisition of wisdom is a moral duty. It's not something you do just to advance in life. And there's a corollary to that idea that is very important. It requires that you're hooked on lifetime learning. Without lifetime learning, you people are not going to do very well... You're going to advance in life by what you learn after you leave here." 伯克希尔："The skill that got Berkshire through one decade would not have sufficed to get it through the next decade with comparable levels of achievement. Warren Buffett had to be a continuous learning machine... I constantly see people rise in life who are not the smartest, sometimes not even the most diligent. But they are learning machines. They go to bed every night a little wiser than they were that morning. And boy, does that habit help, particularly when you have a long run ahead of you." Whitehead："Alfred North Whitehead correctly said... the rapid advance of civilization came only when man 'invented the method of invention.'... Just as civilization can progress only when it invents the method of invention, you can progress only when you learn the method of learning." 巴菲特时间："If you watched him with a time clock, you'd find that about half of his waking time is spent reading. Then a big chunk of the rest of his time is spent talking one-on-one... with highly gifted people whom he trusts and who trust him." 95%："the really big ideas carry about 95 percent of the freight, so it wasn't at all hard for me to pick up about 95 percent of what I needed from all the disciplines and to include use of this knowledge as a standard part of my mental routines." Mirra："He took a sabbatical year... He worked 17 hours a day, seven days a week, for a year. Some sabbatical. At the end of the year, he had created one of the two great bone tumor pathology textbooks of the world."

机制：把学习从'手段'升格为'义务'改变贴现结构——手段在人达成目标后停止，义务终身持续；'每晚聪明一点点'是复利在认知上的形态（长跑里小幅日差异滚成代差）；方法之方法=学习的元技能（芒格的元技能=多学科格栅+清单），比任何具体知识折旧慢；95%载重律=学习成本的帕累托：大观念少而稳定，捡齐它们性价比极高。
迁移：巴菲特时间表可直接抄袭（阅读50%+一对一高质量对话大块）；本塔的canon精读+Field Notes+入塔义务=把'学习机器'制度化的尝试；对公司的审计问题='管理层是学习机器吗：十年前与今天的能力清单对比'；'智慧的道德义务'给投资加一条文化核判据——靠信息优势活着的人（内幕邻近者）在道德账户上是负债者。
