## 原文（Talk Eleven 倾向16）
感知机制："Because the nervous system of man does not naturally measure in absolute scientific units, it must instead rely on something simpler... The contrast in what is seen is registered. And, as in sight, so does it go, largely, in the other senses. Moreover, as perception goes, so goes cognition. The result is man's contrast-misreaction tendency."

小尺度伤害："man's buying an overpriced $1,000 leather dashboard merely because the price is so low compared to his concurrent purchase of a $65,000 car."

大尺度伤害："when a wonderful woman with terrible parents marries a man who would be judged satisfactory only in comparison to her parents."

渐进灾难："When a man's steps are consecutively taken toward disaster, with each step being very small, the brain's contrast-misreaction tendency will often let the man go too far toward disaster to be able to avoid it. This happens because each step presents so small a contrast from his present position."

青蛙："a frog tossed into very hot water would jump out, but the same frog would end up dying if placed in room-temperature water that was later heated at a very slow rate. My few shreds of physiological knowledge make me doubt this account. But no matter, because many businesses die in just the manner claimed by my friend for the frog. Cognition, misled by tiny changes involving low contrast, will often miss a trend that is destiny."

富兰克林："A small leak will sink a great ship." "The utility of the aphorism is large precisely because the brain so often misses the functional equivalent of a small leak in a great ship."

销售滥用："A buyer from out of the city... visits the office with little time available. The salesman deliberately shows the customer three awful houses at ridiculously high prices. Then he shows him a merely bad house at a price only moderately too high. And boom, the broker often makes an easy sale." 假高价折扣："Even when people know that this sort of customer manipulation is being attempted, it will often work to trigger buying... It also demonstrates that being aware of psychological ploys is not a perfect defense."

## 断言
大脑以对比值而非绝对值编码世界，于是对"低对比的连续小变化"系统性失明——每一步与上一步的差都低于警报阈值，整条趋势线却在通向命运。青蛙寓言在生理学上存疑，在组织学上每天应验：生意死亡的方式通常不是断崖而是每季度差一点点的温水。富兰克林"小漏洞沉大船"之所以成为格言，恰因为它对抗的是默认状态。

## 投资迁移
- 财报阅读的基线纪律：单季环比永远显示"还行"，恶化必须拉长基线才显形——毛利率/存货周转/应收/负债率/现金流转化率的5年轨迹图先于单季读数；连续四个"只差一点点"累计起来就是三杀第一层的形成过程。
- 管理层叙事的累计制计分："本季度受一次性因素影响"每出现一次记一分，累计制而非逐季原谅制——借口模式的显影剂就是长基线对比。
- 定价警惕：三烂房战术的企业版=供应商/标的方先展示离谱参照物再报"温和"价格；估值锚（可比公司、历史成交价）的选择权在对手手里时，绝对值计算（DCF、每股现金流）是唯一的防欺诈基线。
- 对自己的组合：净值回撤按累计制监控，"每笔都只亏一点"的连续小损是同一台温水机器。
