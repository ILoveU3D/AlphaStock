## 论证
原文（Talk One）："My second prescription for misery is to learn everything you possibly can from your own experience, minimizing what you learn vicariously from the good and bad experiences of others, living and dead... You can see the results of not learning from others' mistakes by simply looking about you. How little originality there is in the common disasters of mankind: drunk driving deaths, reckless driving maimings, incurable venereal diseases, conversion of bright college students into brainwashed zombies... business failures through repetition of obvious mistakes made by predecessors..." 及Newton例："If I have seen a little farther than other men, it is because I stood on the shoulders of giants."

机制：一手经验的学费昂贵且不可转让（亏掉的钱/健康/岁月），二手经验近零成本但需要'读+信+迁移'三步；多数人卡在信——'我的情况不同'的错觉。灾难不原创说明错误模式是有限集，可编目（正合塔的结构）。

迁移：塔砖就是他人的错误/正确模式的编目库；虚拟盘+复盘=付小学费买大教训的中间层；'这错有人替我犯过了吗'应成为开仓前的标准问题。
