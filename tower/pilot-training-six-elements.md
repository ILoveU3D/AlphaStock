## 论证
原文（Talk Five, HLS 1998）："we train a pilot in a strict six-element system: His formal education is wide enough to cover practically everything useful in piloting. / His knowledge... is not taught just well enough to enable him to pass one test or two; instead, all his knowledge is raised to practice-based fluency, even in handling two or three intertwined hazards at once. / Like any good algebraist, he is made to think sometimes in a forward fashion and sometimes in reverse... / His training time is allocated among subjects so as to minimize damage from his later malfunctions, and so what is most important in his performance gets the most training coverage and is raised to the highest fluency levels. / 'Checklist' routines are always mandatory for him. / Even after original training, he is forced into a special knowledge maintenance routine: regular use of the aircraft simulator to prevent atrophy through long disuse of skills needed to cope with rare and important problems." 根基："The need for this clearly correct six-element system... is rooted in the deep structure of the human mind. Therefore, we must expect that the education we need for broadscale problem-solving will keep all these elements but with awesomely expanded coverage."

机制：六要素各自堵住一个认知失效模式——①堵锤子人（只有一个危险模型就会把一切危险当危险X），②堵'考过即忘'（test-passing≠可用知识），③堵单向思维，④堵平均用力（把训练预算按'失灵代价'而非'学科顺序'分配），⑤堵记忆遗漏（清单外置化工作记忆），⑥堵技能萎缩（罕见场景没有日常练习机会）。为什么飞行行业先做到：飞行员自己坠机——激励与结果完全归己、结果被黑匣子级度量；教育学院的失败对照恰好证明激励结构决定教育质量。
迁移：本工具的全套设计即六要素的投资版——hard gates与红旗清单=⑤，trading多赛季纸交易=⑥（模拟器防手生），三核深评的书面论证义务=②（流利度以'能同时处理交织危害'为标准），误判清单全量主义=①④；个人层面：每季度挑一个罕见但致命的场景做桌面推演（挤兑/断供/大股东减持/技术代际跳变）就是在用模拟器。
