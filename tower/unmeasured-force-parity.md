## 论证
原文（Talk Six）："I agree with Peter Drucker that the culture and legal systems of the United States are especially favorable to shareholder interests compared to other interests and compared to most other countries... This factor, I think, is under-weighed at many investment institutions, probably because it does not easily lead to quantitative thinking using modern financial technique. But some important factor doesn't lose share of force just because some 'expert' can better measure other types of force. Generally, I tend to prefer over direct foreign investment Berkshire's practice of participating in foreign economies through the likes of Coca-Cola and Gillette."

机制：机构的注意力预算被测量工具殖民——能进Excel/因子模型的力获得显性权重，不能进的力被默认为零；这是量化能力对本体论的僭越（'我量得出即存在'的反面：'我量不出即无')；与galileo铁律的真实tension：数学揭示现实≠数学定义现实——伽利略铁律强制'能算必须算'，本砖强制'算不出的不许静默归零'，两者合起来才是完整的认知纪律。
迁移：跨市场投资的显性清单——股东权利位次（A/HK/US三市场的法制与小股东保护是真实折现率项，进估值不进脚注）；本工具A/HK/US三市场分置与港股折价观察的底层变量之一；对'文化'这类L3核心同样适用：文化分数不可精确定量，但按三核平权强制入排序——正是对'量不出的力打折'的制度性反抗。
