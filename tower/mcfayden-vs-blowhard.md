## 论证
原文（Talk Three Q&A）："Grant McFayden treats his employees right, his customers right, and his problems right. And if he gets involved with a psychotic, he quickly walks over to where the psychotic is and works out an exit as fast as he can. Therefore, Grant McFayden doesn't have enough remunerative law business to keep you in Coca-Cola. But Mr. X is a walking minefield of wonderful legal business." 教诲："As you go through life, sell your services once in a while to an unreasonable blowhard if that's what you must do to feed your family. But run your own life like Grant McFayden." 教学法："he told it to me in a way that required a slight mental reach. I had to make the reach myself... And because I had to reach for it, he figured I'd hold it better. And, indeed, I've held it all the way through until today... Good literature makes the reader reach a little for understanding. Then it works better. You hold it better. It's the commitment and consistency tendency. If you've reached for it, the idea's pounded in better." 富兰克林："It's hard for an empty sack to stand upright."

机制：方正经营的人纠纷少=付费业务少（烂人制造业务）；'遇疯子快速找出口'是处理不可理喻方的唯一正解（不纠缠、不对抗、买时间退出）；空麻袋悖论说明为何'偶尔接blowhard的业务'合法——财务独立是行为方正的承重墙（与permanent-capital-structure互证）。

迁移：合作方/客户审计=对方过去十年怎么对待员工、客户、对手+有没有'和精神病缠斗'的历史（缠斗者自己也疯）；D3文化核的创始人审计加一问：他是McFayden型还是blowhard型（对外叙事浮夸度是可观测信号）；遇监管疯狂/恶意诉讼/无理对手：预算一笔'出口成本'快速了结，绝不恋战；教学法用于塔——砖要写成让读者自己'够一下'的形态（statement给断言不给全部论证）。
