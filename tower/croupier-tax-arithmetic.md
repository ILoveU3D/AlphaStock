## 论证
原文（Talk Six, 1998）："All the equity investors, in total, will surely bear a performance disadvantage per annum equal to the total croupiers' costs they have jointly elected to bear. This is an inescapable fact of life. And it is also inescapable that exactly half of the investors will get a result below the median result after the croupiers' take, which median result may well be somewhere between unexciting and lousy." 3%账："the total cost of all the investment management, plus the frictional costs of fairly often getting in and out of many large investment positions, can easily reach 3 percent of foundation net worth per annum... This full cost doesn't show up in conventional accounting. But that is because accounting has limitations and not because the full cost isn't present." 剪刀："if the average annual gross real return from indexed investment in equities goes back, say, to 5 percent over some long future period, and the croupiers' take turns out to remain the waste it has always been... then the average intelligent foundation will be in a prolonged, uncomfortable shrinking mode. After all, 5 percent minus 3 percent minus 5 percent in donations leaves an annual shrinkage of 3 percent." LBO解剖："buying 100 percent of corporations with much financial leverage and two layers of promotional carry... is no sure thing to outperform equity indexes... In substance, an LBO fund is a better way of buying equivalents of marketable equities on margin, and the debt could prove disastrous... there is a buried covariance with marketable equities—toward disaster in generally bad business conditions—and competition is now extreme."

机制：交易是零和、成本是负和——荷官税是唯一确定的先验回报减项，比任何alpha预测的置信度都高；摩擦成本藏在三个盲区（会计不显示、牛市被17%掩盖、委托人看不见别人账户），所以牛市里无人清算，熊市里以缩水显形；LBO的'分散'是表面属性，协方差藏在杠杆与生意条件的交叉项里。
迁移：个人版的荷官税=佣金+印花税+换仓摩擦+滑点+汇率点差——本工具trade模块的费率模型（CITIC/ZA-Bank）就是把这税显形化；指数化判断：'若几乎所有人都转向指数化，它就没法永远好用，但很长一段时间内它会好用'；对'多策略/多管理人'产品先问荷官总抽水率，超过指数毛回报一个数量级的结构直接否决。
