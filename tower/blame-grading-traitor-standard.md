## 论证
原文（Talk Eight）：分析师免责——"I can't come down hardest on low-level cognitive error, much of it subconsciously caused by the standard incentive systems of the world." 官员免责——"these people operate in a virtual maelstrom of regrettable forces and can't reasonably be expected to meet the behavioral standard you seek to impose." 高管定位——"These officers will, of course, get strong punishment for their massive fraud and disgusting stewardship of the great engineer's legacy. But I want you to bring in the miscreants who will soon be in the lowest circle in Hell." 会计判词——"the lowest circle of Hell was reserved for traitors... They occupied high positions in one of the noblest professions, which, like yours, help make society work right by laying down the right rules. They were very smart and securely placed, and it is inexcusable that they deliberately caused all this lying and cheating that was so obviously predictable." 面包与歌："The accounting profession, in making its amazingly peculiar decision, had simply followed the injunction so often followed by persons quite different from prosperous, entrenched accountants. The injunction was that normally followed by insecure and powerless people: 'Whose bread I eat, his song I sing.'"

机制：罪责的四个维度——位置（规则制定者影响全系统）、知识（聪明且securely placed=无不知情的辩护）、蓄意度（明知而为之）、可预测性（'如此明显可预测'）；但丁的地狱分类被芒格挪用：犹大圈（背叛信任者）低于暴力与欺诈圈——因为文明运转依赖规则层被信任，规则层的叛变摧毁的是所有人的合作预期；分析师免责的深层原因：其错误是激励结构的因变量，惩罚因变量不改系统（对应L2红旗清单的制度观：识别比谴责有用）。
迁移：审计公司治理按此分级——看规则由谁制定、制定者吃什么面包：审计委员会与CFO的薪酬结构、评级机构的付费模式、指数编制方的商业利益；'聪明+安稳+明知'的规则层操纵（回购新规、会计准则选择、'行业惯例'话术）是最重红旗；对个人的镜像——我们自己同时是多层规则的制定者（对自己、对家庭、对团队），审视'我定规则时吃了谁的面包'；分析师免责≠分析师无害——免责是审判学的，不是风险学的：跟随分析师共识仍会亏钱。
