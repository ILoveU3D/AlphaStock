## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："you have to know enough about it to understand its limitations—because although accounting is the starting place, it's only a crude approximation... everyone can see that you have to more or less just guess at the useful life of a jet airplane... Just because you express the depreciation rate in neat numbers doesn't make it anything you really know." Braun故事："'This is asinine.' So he threw all of his accountants out... a formidably willful and talented man who demonstrated both the importance of accounting and the importance of knowing its limitations." 五W："You had to tell who was going to do what, where, when, and why. And if you wrote a letter or directive... and you didn't tell him why, you could get fired. In fact, you would get fired if you did it twice." 为什么重要："if you always tell people why, they'll understand it better, they'll consider it more important, and they'll be more likely to comply. Even if they don't understand your reason, they'll be more likely to comply... just as you want to start getting worldly wisdom by asking 'Why, why, why?,' in communicating... you want to include why, why, why."

机制：整洁数字≠知识——会计是经济现实的粗糙投影，把投影当本体是模型错置（锤子人变体）；解释why把服从从盲从变成知情同意，同时提升执行质量与执行概率，还能倒逼指令者想清楚因果。

迁移：DCF模型的输入（永续增长率、折旧年限、终值）全是'整洁数字包装的猜测'——must declare gaps；本工具intel解释层（财报速读/公告含义）存在的制度理由就是五W；写研究备忘录漏掉'为什么'等于Braun级解雇错误；给下属/给自己下的每一条指令都附why。
