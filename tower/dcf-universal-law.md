## 论证
Anything's worth = its future benefit stream discounted by uncertainty. Frame every value trade-off — stocks, careers, skills, health, trust, attention — as duration × certainty. Assets get DCF'd; consumption is enjoyed now; the costliest mistake is buying consumption disguised as an asset. PE and every other metric are just static snapshots of this law; DCF forces the growth path into the open.
