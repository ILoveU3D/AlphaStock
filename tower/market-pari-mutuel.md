## 论证
原文（Talk Two）："the model I like—to sort of simplify the notion of what goes on in a market for common stocks—is the pari-mutuel system at the racetrack... The prices have changed in such a way that it's very hard to beat the system." 极端EMH判词："I have a name for people who went to the extreme efficient market theory, which is 'bonkers'... It just had a difficulty in that the fundamental assumption did not tie properly to reality." 抽水与马客："what a shrewd horseplayer's edge does in most cases is to reduce his average loss over a season of betting from the 17 percent... to maybe 10 percent. However, there are actually a few people who can beat the game after paying the full 17 percent." 股市版："The stock market is the same way, except that the house handle is so much lower... with enough fanaticism and enough discipline, some of the shrewd people are going to get way better results than average." 核心句："It is not given to human beings to have such talent that they can just know everything about everything all the time. But it is given to human beings who work hard at it—who look and sift the world for a mispriced bet—that they can occasionally find one. And the wise ones bet heavily when the world offers them that opportunity. They bet big when they have the odds. And the rest of the time, they don't. It's just that simple." 十洞见："the top 10 insights account for most of it." 打孔卡："a ticket with only 20 slots in it... you'd be forced to load up on what you'd really thought about." 铁律："only 20 percent of the people can be in the top fifth."

机制：价格聚合了大众信息所以'大概有效'，但注意力有限+恐慌/冷落/狂热制造偶发错价；可赢窗口是事件性的，所以优势=筛选的耐心×下注的分量，不是全知全能——行业结构（没人能这样收费）解释了为何机构不照此运营。

迁移：L1宽池+量化只否决的管道本质是彩池错价扫描器；'大多数日子没有值得下注的对象'是策略本体不是失职（空仓等待合法）；recommend输出的唯一pick应该自带'这是20孔卡上的一孔'的分量感；对'再平衡/永远满仓'的默认设置保持彩池式怀疑。
