## 论证
原文（Talk Nine）："This has been attributed to Samuel Johnson... if an academic maintains in place an ignorance that can be easily removed with a little work, the conduct of the academic amounts to treachery... you have a duty if you're an academic to be as little of a klutz as you can possibly be, and therefore you have got to keep grinding out of your system as much removable ignorance as you can remove." 老虎机："That machine is going bar, bar, lemon, bar, bar, grapefruit, way more often than normal machines, and that will cause heavier play... If you know the psychological factors, if you've got them on a checklist in your head, you just run down the factors, and, boom! You get to one that must explain this occurrence. There isn't any other way to do it effectively." 拉美："Where did I read about this case?... It wasn't in the annals of economics. I found this case in the annals of psychology. Clever people went down and used a bunch of psychological tricks. And they fixed it... Why be so ignorant about psychology that you don't even know psychology's tricks that will fix your own dysfunctional economic systems?" 独腿人："If you want to go through life like a one-legged man in an ass-kicking contest, why, be my guest. But if you want to succeed like a strong man with two legs, you have to pick up these methods."

机制：'可除无知'与'不可除无知'的分界线是勤勉——经济学的混乱不可除（普朗克放弃经济学同场），但心理学清单可以读，两者混淆是偷懒的遮羞布；老虎机near miss=变率强化的工程设计版：赌场把'差一点就中'做成参数，利用大脑把near miss误编码为'技能在进步'的错觉；约翰逊判词把无知从'状态'变成'行为'——位置是公共信任，不学即背叛。
迁移：分析里每个'解释不了的现象'先过心理清单再引入阴谋叙事（误判25倾向是第一嫌疑池）；审计产品公司时的'成瘾工程'清单——near miss设计/变量奖励/限时 scarcity 既是红旗也是商业威力来源（游戏、短视频、博彩机制股按此双读）；对本塔的义务：每块砖写明可证伪条件，不可证伪的断言=可除无知。
